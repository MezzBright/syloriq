Meet Our Founder

Mezz Bright

Founder & CEO

Mezz Bright is a visionary entrepreneur and digital innovator, serving as the Founder and President of SYLORIQ — a full-spectrum brand dedicated to business growth through technology, design, marketing, and strategy. With a passion for building transformative digital solutions, Mezz leads the team with a clear mission: to empower businesses and creators with smart, scalable, and impactful digital tools. Driven by creativity, strategy, and execution, he oversees the development of SYLORIQ’s services — from web development and digital marketing to branding, content, and business consultation. As the brain behind the vision, Mezz is committed to building a global tech-driven brand that redefines how brands grow, connect, and thrive in the digital age.
